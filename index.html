Day by day

Every day carries the bed it ends in.

  1. Fri 2 Oct

    Arrive

    Bologna airport hotel

    Land 23:40. Nothing else.

  2. Sat 3 Oct

    Bologna, slow

    Bologna

    Piazza Maggiore, the Quadrilatero market — Saturday is its best day — the seven churches of Santo Stefano, and the Torre degli Asinelli climb on a timed ticket. Long dinner.

    Museums & architecture

    • Basilica di San Petronio

      The huge brick face on Piazza Maggiore, left unfinished in the 1500s and never resolved. Inside, Cassini's 1655 meridian line is inlaid the length of the floor.

    • Archiginnasio & Teatro Anatomico

      First permanent home of the university. Walls papered in carved student coats of arms, and a 1637 anatomical theatre in spruce — reconstructed from the pieces after a 1944 bomb.

    • Santa Maria della Vita

      Niccolò dell'Arca's terracotta Lamentation: six life-size mourners caught mid-cry. Ten minutes, and the best thing in the city.

    • Pinacoteca Nazionale

      The Bolognese school at home — Carracci, Reni, Guercino — plus Raphael's Ecstasy of St Cecilia.

    • MAMbo & Museo Morandi

      Modern and contemporary art in the old municipal bakery. Morandi's bottles are upstairs.

    • The porticoes

      UNESCO-listed since 2021. The San Luca one climbs 3.8 km under 666 arches to the sanctuary on the hill — a half-day on its own.

    More than one day holds. Santa Maria della Vita, the Archiginnasio and San Petronio are the short list; they sit within a few minutes of each other.

    No car yet. The centre is one large ZTL.

  3. Sun 4 Oct

    North

    Ortisei first of three nights

    Collect the rental at BLQ in the morning and drive to Ortisei — about 4 h on the A22 over the Brenner corridor. Afternoon in Val Gardena.

  4. Mon 5 Oct

    Seceda

    Ortisei

    The two-stage cableway from Ortisei via Furnes to roughly 2,500 m. Go up early for the ridge, before the light flattens.

    Season ends 11 Oct. Inside the window, but there is no slack.

  5. Tue 6 Oct

    Alpe di Siusi & Val di Funes

    Ortisei last night in the mountains

    Take the Compatsch gondola; the road up is closed to cars during the day. Afternoon at Santa Maddalena in Val di Funes for the classic church-and-Odle view.

    Swap for the Sella / Pordoi / Carezza pass loop, or Lago di Braies and Tre Cime, if the forecast favours it.

  6. Wed 7 Oct

    South to Florence

    Florence first of three nights

    Drive back to Bologna, about 4 h, drop the car at the station, train to Florence, about 35 min. Sunset at Piazzale Michelangelo.

  7. Thu 8 Oct

    The big rooms

    Florence

    The headline collections, all on timed tickets. One gallery in the morning, the Duomo complex in the afternoon.

    Museums & architecture

    • Uffizi book ahead

      Botticelli, Leonardo, and the Raphael and Titian rooms, in Vasari's office block along the Arno.

    • Accademia book ahead

      The David — and the unfinished Prisoners straining out of their blocks in the corridor before it.

    • Duomo complex book ahead

      Brunelleschi's dome, Giotto's campanile and the Baptistery on one pass. The dome climb is a separate reserved slot — 463 steps, no lift.

    • Museo dell'Opera del Duomo

      Where the originals went: Ghiberti's Gates of Paradise panels, Donatello's Magdalene, Michelangelo's Bandini Pietà. Quiet, and usually empty.

    • Bargello

      Sculpture in a 13th-century palace and prison. Donatello's two Davids, and the Ghiberti–Brunelleschi competition panels side by side.

    Uffizi and Accademia on the same day is a lot of standing. Take one now and the other tomorrow.

  8. Fri 9 Oct

    Florence, slower

    Florence

    Oltrarno and the artisan workshops, then Boboli or Bardini for the afternoon.

    Museums & architecture

    • Brancacci Chapel book ahead

      Masaccio's frescoes — the moment painting worked out perspective and grief at the same time. Small timed groups, so it goes early.

    • Santo Spirito

      Brunelleschi's last church. The front is bare plaster; the argument is all inside, in the colonnade.

    • Palazzo Pitti & Palatine Gallery

      Raphaels and Titians hung frame-to-frame up the wall, the way a grand duke would have had them. Boboli is the same ticket.

    • Medici Chapels & Laurentian Library

      Michelangelo twice at San Lorenzo: the New Sacristy with Night and Day, and the library vestibule staircase that pours down at you.

    • Santa Croce

      Michelangelo, Galileo and Machiavelli in the nave; Brunelleschi's Pazzi Chapel out in the cloister.

    • Santa Maria Novella

      Alberti's façade — the first to solve the wide-nave-narrow-aisles problem with scrolls. Masaccio's Trinity on the left wall.

    An Oltrarno morning plus two of these is a full day. Don't try for four.

    Optional day out to Siena and Chianti instead.

  9. Sat 10 Oct

    The coast

    Levanto or Monterosso · two nights

    Train to La Spezia, about 2.5 h, usually changing in Pisa, then onward to the base. Afternoon village-hopping on the Cinque Terre Express.

  10. Sun 11 Oct

    Cinque Terre, full day

    Levanto or Monterosso

    Walk Vernazza → Monterosso, 1.5–2 h, steep in places and easier in that direction. Via dell'Amore in a booked slot. Train between the rest.

  11. Mon 12 Oct

    Home

    No bed flight 18:40

    Late-morning train La Spezia → Bologna, about 2–2.5 h. Lunch in Bologna, then out to the airport.

    It's a Monday, so the Pinacoteca and MAMbo are shut. San Petronio, Santo Stefano and the porticoes are open — churches and streets, not museums.

    Keep the afternoon as buffer.

03

Transport

Car for 4–7 October only

Pick up and return at Bologna, so there is no one-way fee. The Dolomites genuinely need a car. Nothing else on this route does.

Why not keep it

Florence's ZTL is camera-enforced and the fines arrive months later through the rental company. Garage parking runs €25–35 a day. The Cinque Terre villages are ZTL with small, expensive lots. Nine rental days to use it for three.

Cinque Terre trains

The Cinque Terre Express runs every 15–20 min along La Spezia – Riomaggiore – Manarola – Corniglia – Vernazza – Monterosso – Levanto. Village to village is 3–8 min. The Cinque Terre Treno Card covers unlimited rides.

Base choice on the coast

Central is not a requirement.

Levanto
Flat, cheaper, restaurants that aren't tourist-priced. 5 min by train to Monterosso.
La Spezia
Cheapest, most rooms, best rail connections. 8 min to Riomaggiore.
Monterosso
Most convenient inside the park, and the only village without punishing stairs — which matters with suitcases.

05

Things to know

Seceda cableway season
Ortisei–Furnes–Seceda runs 16.05.–11.10.2026, and the Gardena Card is valid to 11.10.2026 as well. Everything mountain-related has to happen before that date.
Alpe di Siusi road
Closed to cars during daytime hours. Use the Compatsch gondola.
Cinque Terre Card
Required 14 March – 2 November 2026.
Via dell'Amore
Needs a booked 30-minute slot, is one-way Riomaggiore → Manarola, and is included in the standard card as of March 2026. Slots are not reliably refundable — check the forecast the day before and commit.
Trail status
Manarola–Corniglia stays closed long-term. Vernazza–Monterosso is fully open and is the stretch worth walking. Corniglia–Vernazza is also open.
Museum closing days
Most Italian state museums shut on Mondays — the Uffizi, the Accademia, Bologna's Pinacoteca and MAMbo among them. The city days here are a Saturday and a Thursday–Friday, so they are all clear; only the fly-out Monday is affected. Churches keep their own hours and generally stay open.
Weather
Early October in the Dolomites can bring first snow above 2,000 m, and daylight is getting short. The cities stay mild. Pack for both.
ZTL
Applies in the Bologna and Florence centres. Never drive in.
